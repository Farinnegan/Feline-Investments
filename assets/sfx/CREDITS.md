# Sound credits

All sounds in this folder come from Kenney (www.kenney.nl) and are released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0): free to use, no attribution required. Credit given anyway.

Packs used: Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, Casino Audio, Music Jingles,
Voiceover Pack. Converted to mono mp3 and loudness-matched for the game.

These files load from GitHub Pages and are not stored by the game's offline cache.
